💡 What can I do?

If you see “region not supported”, try these tips:
🛡️ Turn off VPN / Proxy
The app checks your real location. If you’re using a VPN or proxy, please disable it.
  • On PC: Close your VPN application or turn off proxy in network settings.
  • On mobile: Go to Settings → VPN and disable any active connection.
  • Try reloading the page (F5 or refresh).
🧹 Clear cache and cookies
Sometimes your browser stores old data that may block access.
  • Chrome / Edge: Ctrl+Shift+Delete → select “Cookies and cached files” → Clear.
  • Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+Delete → select “Cookies and Cache” → OK.
  • After clearing, refresh the page.
🌐 Check DNS settings
Some DNS servers might hide your actual location.
  • Use your ISP’s default DNS (automatic).
  • Or try public DNS like Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) or Google (8.8.8.8).
  • DNS changes can be made in your network / Wi-Fi settings.
📱 Try a different connection
Your current network might be blocked.
  • Switch from Wi-Fi to mobile data (or vice versa).
  • If you’re on a corporate network, try connecting through your home Wi-Fi.
  • Restart your router / modem.
🕒 Wait and try again
Sometimes it’s a temporary outage or a geo‑location check.
  • Wait 10–15 minutes and refresh the page.
  • If the issue persists, please contact our support.
