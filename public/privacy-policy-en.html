1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "policy") describes how the independent TrueVibe team processes your personal data when you use our TrueVibe photo-sharing platform (hereinafter referred to as the "service" or "platform").

Our processing of personal data is in compliance with:

  • Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR)
  • Act No. 18/2018 Coll. on Personal Data Protection and on Amendments to Certain Acts
  • Act No. 351/2011 Coll. on Electronic Communications, as amended

2. What Personal Data We Process

2.1 Data you provide directly:

  • Username: A unique username you choose during registration
  • Email address: For account verification, password recovery, and important notifications
  • Password: Stored in the form of a secure hash (encrypted)
  • Photos and visual content: Photos you upload and share on the platform
  • Profile information: Optional information you add to your profile (bio, avatar)
  • Communication content: Messages, comments, and interactions with other users

2.2 Data we collect automatically:

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system version
  • Device data: Device type, unique device identifiers
  • Log data: Time and date of login, activity history on the platform
  • Photo metadata: Creation date, file size, format (EXIF data according to your settings)
  • Cookies and similar technologies: More information can be found in our Cookie Policy

3. Purposes of Processing Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Providing the service: Creating and managing your user account, authentication, maintaining your login session
  • Content sharing: Enabling the publishing, storing, and sharing of your photos with other users
  • Personalization: Tailoring your feed, recommending content based on your activities and preferences
  • Communication: Sending important notifications about the service, updates, and security alerts
  • Security: Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and protecting your account
  • Service improvement: Analyzing usage, debugging errors, developing new features
  • Legal compliance: Fulfilling our legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds under Article 6 of the GDPR:

4.1 Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)

We process data necessary to provide the TrueVibe service, including creating your account, publishing photos, and using the platform's core features.

4.2 Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

We process data for the purposes of network and information security, preventing abuse, improving the service, and ensuring the technical stability of the platform. Our legitimate interest does not override your rights and freedoms.

4.3 Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)

For certain purposes, such as marketing communications (if offered) or advanced personalization, we process data based on your explicit consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

4.4 Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR)

If required by law, we process data to comply with our legal obligations.

5. How Long We Retain Your Data

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Account data: For the duration of your account and up to 30 days after deletion (for possible recovery)
  • Published photos: Until you delete them or close your account
  • Log data: Maximum of 12 months from creation
  • Email address after account deletion: Up to 3 years to prevent re-registration of abusive accounts
  • Data required for legal obligations: According to relevant legal regulations (typically 5-10 years)

After these periods expire, we will securely delete or anonymize your data.

Important Information About Photos

Photos you publish on TrueVibe are visible to other users according to your privacy settings. Even after deletion from the platform, photos may be stored in backups for a maximum of 90 days. Please note that other users may download or share photos outside our platform – in such cases, we have no control over further distribution.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share data in the following cases:

6.1 Service providers (processors)

We work with trusted service providers who help us operate TrueVibe:

  • Hosting and cloud services: Providers of server capacity and storage
  • Email delivery: Services for sending transactional emails
  • Analytics tools: Services for usage analysis (with anonymization)
  • Payment gateways: If we offer paid features
  • CDN (Content Delivery Network): For fast loading of photos

All our providers are bound by contractual data protection obligations and may not use your data for other purposes.

6.2 Public authorities

We may provide data to law enforcement or other state authorities if required by law or if necessary to protect our rights, user safety, or prevent fraud.

6.3 In case of change of ownership

If our TrueVibe team were to merge with another entity or undergo an asset transfer, users will be notified in advance and their data will be protected under this policy.

7. International Data Transfers

Our services may use infrastructure located outside the European Economic Area (EEA). In such cases, we ensure adequate safeguards for your data through:

  • European Commission adequacy decisions
  • Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Other appropriate security measures under Article 46 of the GDPR

8. Security of Your Data

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption: Communication between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Secure password storage: Passwords are stored as secure hashes using modern algorithms
  • Regular backups: Regular secure data backups
  • Limited access: Access to personal data is restricted to authorized team members
  • Security updates: Regular software and security patch updates

Note: No method of data transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Despite our efforts to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

9. Your Rights

As a data subject, you have the following rights under the GDPR:

9.1 Right of access

You have the right to obtain confirmation from us as to whether we process your personal data, and if so, the right to access that data and information about how it is processed.

9.2 Right to rectification

You have the right to have inaccurate personal data corrected or completed if it is incomplete.

9.3 Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")

You have the right to have your personal data erased if:

  • The data is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected
  • You withdraw your consent and there is no other legal ground for processing
  • You object to the processing and there are no overriding legitimate grounds
  • The data has been unlawfully processed

9.4 Right to restriction of processing

You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your data in certain situations (e.g., when you contest the accuracy of the data).

9.5 Right to data portability

You have the right to receive your personal data provided to us in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format, and the right to transmit that data to another controller.

9.6 Right to object

You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.

9.7 Right to withdraw consent

If we process your data based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

9.8 Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic if you believe that the processing of your data violates the GDPR.
