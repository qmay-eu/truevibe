TrueVibe Help Center

📌 How to use TrueVibe

  • Sign up / log in
  • Create a post (text + image)
  • Likes ❤️ and reposts 🔁
  • Notifications – allow them in your browser
  • Switch between dark / light mode
  • Tap an image to view it fullscreen

❓ Common issues

  • Forgotten password: password recovery is not available yet – please contact support.
  • Image won't upload: max 2 MB, formats JPG / PNG / GIF / WEBP.
  • Notifications not arriving: enable them in your browser settings.
  • Post not showing up: refresh the page or check your connection.

⚙️ Tips & tricks

  • Use your personalised feed – like what interests you.
  • Repost with a comment – it increases your reach.
  • Upload images in good quality but keep them under 2 MB.
  • If something isn't working, try clearing your cache (CTRL+F5).
