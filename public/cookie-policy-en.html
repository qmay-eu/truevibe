Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored in your browser or on your device when you visit a website or use an application. These files help us provide better services, remember your preferences, and improve the functionality of the TrueVibe platform.

Legal Basis

The use of cookies on the TrueVibe platform is in compliance with:

  • GDPR Regulation (Articles 6 and 7)
  • Act No. 351/2011 Coll. on Electronic Communications (§ 55 et seq.)
  • Act No. 18/2018 Coll. on Personal Data Protection
  • ePrivacy Directive (2002/58/EC as amended by 2009/136/EC)

Under Slovak legislation, we require your consent before using any cookies that are not strictly necessary for the operation of the service.

Types of Cookies We Use

We categorize cookies into the following groups:

1. Essential Cookies

No consent required

These cookies are strictly necessary for the basic functioning of the TrueVibe platform. Without them, the service could not function properly. These cookies are stored automatically because they are needed to:

  • Maintain your login session while using the service
  • Ensure security (e.g., protection against CSRF attacks)
  • Store your privacy settings and cookie preferences
  • Remember whether you have accepted or rejected cookies
  • Technically enable content loading

Legal basis: Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – technical necessity for providing the service.

2. Functional Cookies

Consent required

These cookies allow us to remember your choices and provide enhanced, personalized features:

  • Storing language preferences
  • Remembering display settings (dark/light mode)
  • Storing media playback preferences
  • Personalizing the interface according to your habits

Legal basis: Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

3. Analytics Cookies

Consent required

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the TrueVibe platform. The information collected is anonymous or pseudonymized and we use it to:

  • Measure traffic and service usage
  • Analyze which features are most popular
  • Identify technical issues and errors
  • Improve the user experience
  • Test new features (A/B testing)

Legal basis: Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) or legitimate interest for anonymized analytics (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Marketing Cookies

Consent required

If we offer targeted advertising or marketing campaigns in the future, these cookies would be used to:

  • Track the effectiveness of marketing campaigns
  • Limit the number of ad impressions
  • Measure the success of advertising activities

Legal basis: Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Note: Currently, TrueVibe does not use marketing cookies or third-party targeted advertising.

How to Manage Your Cookie Preferences

Granting and Rejecting Consent

Upon your first visit to the TrueVibe platform, you will see a banner allowing you to choose which cookies you want to allow. You have the following options:

  • Accept all: You consent to all categories of cookies
  • Reject all: You reject all non-essential cookies (only essential cookies will be enabled)
  • Settings: You choose specific categories of cookies you want to allow

You can change your selection at any time.

Changing Cookie Settings

You can change your cookie preferences at any time in the following ways:

1. Directly in the TrueVibe app

  • Open Settings
  • Find the Privacy and Security section
  • Click on Cookie Settings
  • Adjust your preferences and save changes

2. Through your browser

Most web browsers allow you to manage cookies through their settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

Please note that if you disable all cookies in your browser, some TrueVibe features may not function properly.

3. Deleting cookies

You can delete cookies from your device at any time through your browser settings. After deleting cookies, you will need to set your preferences again on your next visit.

Technologies Similar to Cookies

In addition to cookies, we use other similar technologies:

Local Storage and Session Storage

These technologies allow us to store larger amounts of data directly in your browser. We use them for:

  • Temporarily storing photos before upload
  • Storing extended application settings
  • Offline functionality (if available)

Pixel tags (web beacons)

We may use small graphic elements (1x1 pixel) to track interactions with email messages (opening an email, clicking a link). These technologies help us improve our communication with you.

Third-Party Cookies

Our platform may contain content or services provided by third parties that may set their own cookies:

  • CDN (Content Delivery Network): For faster loading of photos and content
  • Video hosting: If we integrate videos from external sources
  • Social media: Sharing buttons (if implemented)
  • Analytics services: External analytics tools (with your consent)

These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we have no control over their cookies. We recommend that you review their privacy policies.

Consequences of Rejecting Cookies

If you reject non-essential cookies:

  • ✅ Core TrueVibe features will still work (login, publishing photos)
  • ✅ Your privacy will be better protected
  • ⚠️ Some enhanced features may not be available
  • ⚠️ The interface may not adapt to your preferences
  • ⚠️ We cannot analyze service usage to improve it
